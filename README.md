# Returns & Refunds: concept prototype

A concept for an AI-assisted returns and refunds workflow in a merchant dashboard. The AI gathers missing evidence, checks the claim against the merchant's policy and recommends an outcome; the merchant makes every decision.

**Not an official Razorpay product.** All merchants, customers, orders, payment IDs and photos are mock data. AI findings and refunds are simulated. No real money moves.

The app runs entirely in your browser. Your changes are saved only in your browser; use **Reset demo** to start again.
